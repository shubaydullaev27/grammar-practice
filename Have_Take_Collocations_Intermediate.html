Grammar Summary

Have & Take Collocations

"Have" and "take" both combine with certain nouns to form fixed expressions. The meaning depends on the whole phrase, not the verb alone — so it helps to learn each collocation as a single chunk.

Collocations with take

CollocationMeaning
take advantage (of)make use of an opportunity
take placehappen, occur
take turnsdo something one after another, alternating
take it for grantedassume something without question
take something seriouslytreat something as important
take my/your word for itbelieve someone without proof
take care oflook after

Collocations with have

CollocationMeaning
have a feeling (that)sense or suspect something
have a clueknow / understand (usually "not have a clue")
have a word withspeak briefly to someone
have difficulty (doing something)find something hard to do
