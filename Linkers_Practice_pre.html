✨ Study guide

Linkers for surprising information

Some linkers tell us about things that are not what we expect. Study the meaning, the grammar pattern and the example of each linker, then practise.

Translations:

GRAMMAR How to use them

Step 1 · Look at what comes after the linker

🧩
A full clausesubject + verb→ although / even though
📦
A noun or -ingthe rain / being ill→ despite / in spite of
✂️
A new sentence. However, …→ however / instead

Step 2 · Learn the pattern

RULE 1
Although / Even though
Pattern
Although+subject + verb,main clause
Example
Although he was tired, he worked.
Common mistake
✗Although the rain, we played.
✓Although it rained, we played.
Translation
UZgarchi … boʻlsa-da, …ga qaramay
RUхотя
💡 Even though is stronger than although. Both need a full clause.
RULE 2
Despite / In spite of
Pattern
Despite+noun / -ing,main clause
Example
Despite being tired, he worked.
In spite of the rain, we played.
Common mistake
✗Despite of the rain, we played.
✓Despite the rain, we played.
✓In spite of the rain, we played.
Translation
UZ…ga qaramay
RUнесмотря на
💡 Same meaning. Only in spite of has “of”; despite never does.
RULE 3
But
Pattern
clause 1,butclause 2
Example
He was tired, but he worked.
Common mistake
✗He was tired, but although he worked.
✓He was tired, but he worked.
Translation
UZlekin, ammo, biroq
RUно, однако
💡 Use only one linker. But goes in the middle, with a comma before it.
RULE 4
However / Instead
Pattern
sentence 1.However,sentence 2
Example
He was tired. However, he worked.
He didn’t rest. Instead, he worked.
Common mistake
✗He was tired, however he worked.
✓He was tired. However, he worked.
Translation
UZhowever: biroq · instead: buning oʻrniga
RUhowever: однако · instead: вместо этого
💡 However = surprising contrast. Instead = one thing in place of another.

WORDS Meaning and usage

↔️but
clause 1, but clause 2
UZlekin, ammo, biroq
RUно, однако
DEFINITIONJoins two ideas in one sentence when the second one is surprising or opposite to the first.
The soup looked simple, but it tasted wonderful.
🌦️although
Although + subject + verb, …
UZgarchi … boʻlsa-da, …ga qaramay
RUхотя
DEFINITIONIntroduces a fact that is surprising compared with the main idea. It is followed by a full clause.
Although it was cold, we ate outside.
💪even though
Even though + subject + verb, …
UZgarchi … boʻlsa ham (although’dan kuchliroq)
RUхотя (и), несмотря на то что (сильнее, чем although)
DEFINITIONA stronger form of although. It stresses that the first fact makes the result even more surprising.
Even though he is very shy, he loves public speaking.
🚧despite
Despite + noun / -ing, …
UZ…ga qaramay
RUнесмотря на
DEFINITIONShows contrast. It is followed by a noun or an -ing form, NOT by a full clause. Never say “despite of”.
Despite the noise, she fell asleep quickly.
🛑in spite of
In spite of + noun / -ing, …
UZ…ga qaramay (despite bilan bir xil)
RUнесмотря на (то же, что despite)
DEFINITIONHas the same meaning and grammar as despite. It is followed by a noun or an -ing form.
We enjoyed the trip in spite of the rain.
🔄instead
Sentence 1. Instead, sentence 2.
UZbuning oʻrniga
RUвместо этого
DEFINITIONShows that one thing happened in place of the thing we expected or planned.
I didn’t call him. Instead, I sent a message.
⚡however
Sentence 1. However, sentence 2.
UZbiroq, shunga qaramay
RUоднако, тем не менее
DEFINITIONStarts a new sentence and gives surprising or opposite information. Put a comma after it.
The bag was heavy. However, she carried it alone.
Tip: although / even though + FULL CLAUSE; despite / in spite of + NOUN or -ING; however / instead start a NEW SENTENCE.UZ: although / even though + toʻliq gap; despite / in spite of + ot yoki -ing; however / instead — yangi gapni boshlaydi.
RU: although / even though + полное предложение; despite / in spite of + существительное или -ing; however / instead начинают новое предложение.
