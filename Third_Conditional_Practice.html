Grammar Summary

The Third Conditional

Used to talk about imaginary or unreal situations in the past — how the past could have been different if something had happened differently.

Structure
If Past Perfect , would / could / might have + Past Participle
= If + had + V³, would have + V³
If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.
If they had left earlier, they wouldn't have missed the train.
If she had brought a map, we could have found the hotel.

Modal verbs in the third conditional

would have + V³

Expresses the most likely result.

If I had seen you, I would have spoken to you.

could have + V³

Expresses possibility or ability.

If we had left earlier, we could have caught the bus.

might have + V³

Expresses a possible result.

If he had taken the medicine, he might have felt better.
