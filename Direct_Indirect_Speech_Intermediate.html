Grammar Summary

Direct & Indirect Questions

Direct questions ask directly, with the verb before the subject. Indirect questions ask politely, hidden inside a phrase like "Do you know…?" — and keep normal statement word order.

Direct

Question word order — the verb (or auxiliary) comes before the subject.

Where does he live?
What did she buy?
Is he ready?
Indirect

Statement word order — the subject comes before the verb, after an introductory phrase.

Do you know where he lives?
Can you tell me what she bought?
Do you know if he is ready?

Key rules

WH-question
Keep the question word.Where does he work? → Do you know where he works?
Yes / No
Add "if" or "whether".Is she coming? → Do you know if she is coming?
⚠️ No inversion in indirect questions: Do you know where does he live? → Do you know where he lives?
DIRECT= question order | INDIRECT= statement order
