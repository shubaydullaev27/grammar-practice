1. The main idea

We use the present perfect for a situation that started in the past and continues now.

since 2015the starting point
for 11 yearsthe length of time
NOW
Sarah has lived in London since 2015.
Sarah has lived in London for 11 years.
She lived there in 2015, and she still lives there now.

Remember
  • I / you / we / they → have.  He / she / it / one person or thing → has.
  • Short forms and full forms are both correct: She’s lived here = She has lived here. He hasn’t called = He has not called.

5. Common mistakes

  • ✘ I live here since 2015.  ✔ I have lived here since 2015.
  • ✘ for 2015 / since five years  →  ✔ since 2015 / for five years
  • ✘ since two years ago  →  ✔ for two years

6. How to write the sentences

Example: Mary / be a teacher / 2004 → Mary has been a teacher since 2004.

  1. Start with the subject. Add has / have (negative → hasn’t / haven’t).
  2. Change the verb into the past participle.
  3. Add the rest, then for (length of time) or since (starting point).
