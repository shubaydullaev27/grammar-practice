Why sleep matters

Many people do not get enough sleep. Students stay up late to study or to use their phones, and workers often sleep only five or six hours a night. Doctors say that most adults need seven to nine hours of sleep, and teenagers need even more.

Sleep is very important for our body. While we sleep, our body rests and repairs itself. People who sleep well get ill less often, because sleep helps the body fight germs. Sleep is also good for the heart and helps us keep a healthy weight.

Sleep is important for our brain, too. When we are tired, it is difficult to concentrate, and we forget things easily. Students who sleep well usually get better marks, because the brain stores new information during the night. Tired people can also be in a bad mood and get angry quickly.

There are some simple ways to sleep better. First, go to bed and get up at the same time every day. Second, do not drink coffee or tea in the evening, because they keep you awake. Finally, turn off your phone an hour before bed. The light from the screen makes it harder to fall asleep.
