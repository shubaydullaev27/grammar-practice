Grammar Summary

Reported Speech

We use reported speech to tell someone what another person said. The verb tense usually shifts one step back into the past.

Structure
Subject + said / told + (that) + clause, one tense back
“I am tired.” → He said (that) he was tired.
“I will see you tomorrow.” → He said that he would see me the next day.
“Don't be late.” → She told me not to be late.
“Please help me.” → He asked me to help him.

Six shifts in action

Present Simple → Past Simple

Direct: "I work here."

Reported: He said (that) he worked there.

Present Continuous → Past Continuous

Direct: "I am studying."

Reported: She said (that) she was studying.

Present Perfect → Past Perfect

Direct: "I have finished."

Reported: He said (that) he had finished.

Past Simple → Past Perfect

Direct: "I lost my keys."

Reported: She said she had lost her keys.

Will → Would

Direct: "I will call you."

Reported: He said that he would call me.

Can → Could

Direct: "I can swim."

Reported: She said she could swim.

Grammar Summary

Reported Questions

We use reported questions to tell someone what another person asked. Word order becomes normal statement order — no auxiliary inversion.

Two patterns
Yes/No Questions
if / whether + subject + verb
Wh- Questions
question word + subject + verb
Yes/No questions use if/whether. Wh- questions keep the question word.
“Are you tired?” → He asked me if I was tired.
“Where do you live?” → She asked me where I lived.
❌ She asked me where did I live. → no question word order
✅ She asked me where I lived.

Step 1 — Identify the type of question

Wh- Questions

Keep the question word (what, where, when, why, who, how…).

asked + Question word + Subject + Verb

▸ "Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived.

▸ "Why were you late?" → The teacher asked why I had been late.

Yes/No Questions

Use if or whether.

asked + if / whether + Subject + Verb

▸ "Are you busy?" → He asked if I was busy.

▸ "Can you swim?" → She asked whether I could swim.

Step 2 — Change the word order

Use statement word order in reported questions.

Direct: Question word + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb — Where do you live?

Reported: Question word + Subject + Verb

✗ He asked where did I live.

✓ He asked where I lived.
