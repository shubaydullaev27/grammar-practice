Grammar Summary

Passive Voice

Passive Voice is used when the action is more important than the person who does it.

Structure
Object + be + Past Participle (V3) + (by + agent)
The agent (by …) is often omitted if it is unknown or unimportant.
The committee chose a new leader. → A new leader was chosen (by the committee).
People speak English in many countries. → English is spoken in many countries.
They will announce the results tomorrow. → The results will be announced tomorrow.

Common passive tenses

am / is / are + V³

Present Simple Passive — general facts and routines.

English is spoken in many countries.

was / were + V³

Past Simple Passive — a completed action in the past.

The Microsoft Corporation was started by Bill Gates.

have / has been + V³

Present Perfect Passive — the result is visible now.

A website has been set up for new companies.

am / is / are / was / were + being + V³

Continuous Passive — an action in progress (present or past).

The road is being repaired at the moment.

modal + be + V³

Modal Passive — with can, must, should, will, may, might, etc.

All applications must be reviewed by the bank.

Any modal (can, could, must, should, will, would, may, might) can also combine with the perfect or continuous passive, e.g. should have been finished, must be being tested.
