1. The main rule

After a time word (when, if, as soon as, before, after, until) we talk about the future with the present simple, not with will.

time word + present simplethe time clause
,
will + base verbthe main clause
As soon as the sun comes up, we ’ll start our walk.
✔ When I arrive, I’ll call you.  ✘ When I will arrive, I’ll call you.

2. Word order and comma

Main clause first → no comma I’ll look at the map before we go.
Time clause first → comma ( , ) Before we go, I’ll look at the map.
✘ I will take the photo. After I put it on the internet.
✔ After I take the photo, I’ll put it on the internet.
Never separate the two clauses with a full stop.

4. Before or after?

After + the action that happens first After I take the photo, I’ll put it online.
1st: take the photo → 2nd: put it online
Before + the action that happens second I’ll put it online before I go to bed.
1st: put it online → 2nd: go to bed

Clue words: comes first, first … then, do it first.

5. Quick check

  1. Is it sure? → when.  Not sure? → if.
  2. Does it happen immediately? → as soon as.  Up to a moment (waiting)? → until.
  3. Order of events: which action is first? → after + first action, before + second action.
  4. Then always: present simple after the time word, will + base verb in the other clause.
