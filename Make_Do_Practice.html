✨ Study guide

DO or MAKE?

Learn the phrases first, then practice. These word combinations are fixed, so learn each one as a whole phrase.

Translations:
DO

Use do for tasks, jobs, duties and activities. Also for “nothing”.

UZDO — vazifa, ish va mashgʻulotlar uchun: uy vazifasi, uy ishlari, hech narsa.

RUDO — для заданий, работы и повседневных дел: домашнее задание, работа по дому, ничего.

I always do my homework after school.
MAKE

Use make to create or produce something, and for plans, decisions, mistakes and sounds.

UZMAKE — biror narsani yaratish, tayyorlash uchun; reja, qaror, xato, shovqin.

RUMAKE — создавать, производить; планы, решения, ошибки, звуки.

We made a plan for the trip.
DOdo · did · doneMAKEmake · made · made
