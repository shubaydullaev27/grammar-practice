✨ Study guide

Learn first

Some verbs always go with a fixed preposition. Learn the verb and the preposition together.

Translations:
Verb + preposition

Many verbs need a fixed preposition: depend on, laugh at, belong to. The wrong preposition changes the meaning or sounds wrong.

UZBa'zi fe'llar doimiy predlog bilan ishlatiladi. Fe'l va predlogni birga yodlang.

RUУ некоторых глаголов всегда один и тот же предлог. Учите глагол и предлог вместе.

We agree with our teacher.
After the preposition

After a preposition use a noun or the -ing form: succeed in passing, dream of traveling.

UZPredlogdan keyin ot yoki -ing shakli keladi.

RUПосле предлога ставьте существительное или форму -ing.

She dreams of becoming a pilot.

Phrasal Verbs

16 tasks, 90 questions. Read the guide, then practice. Type the answer or tap an option, then press Enter or “Check”.

✨ Study guide

Learn first

A phrasal verb is a verb + a small word. Its meaning is often different from the words alone.

Translations:
Phrasal verb = verb + particle

give up does not mean “give” + “up”. It means stop. Learn each phrasal verb as one new word.

UZFrazal fe'l = fe'l + qoʻshimcha. Ma'nosi alohida soʻzlardan farq qiladi.

RUФразовый глагол = глагол + частица. Значение часто новое: give up = бросить.

He gave up sugar last year.
Forms

Change the verb, not the small word: grow up → grew up, find out → found out, take up → took up.

UZFe'lning shaklini oʻzgartiring, qoʻshimchani emas.

RUМеняйте глагол, а не частицу: grow up → grew up.

She took up dancing.
