Word Formation

Adjectives, Adverbs & Nouns

Many words change form depending on their job in the sentence — an adjective describes a noun, an adverb describes a verb, and a noun names a thing or quality. Recognising the pattern helps you pick the right form.

beautiful→beautifully
adj → adv
dramatic→dramatically
adj → adv
honest→honesty
adj → noun
happy→happiness
adj → noun
care→careful→carefully
noun → adj → adv
week→weekly
noun → adj/adv
near→nearby
adv/adj
fresh→freshly
adj → adv
like→likely
adj, "probable"
remarkable→remarkably
adj → adv

Common mistakes

❌highly possible→✅highly likely
❌fresh painted→✅freshly painted
❌be carefully→✅be careful
❌dramatic increased→✅increased dramatically
