Grammar Summary

Used to / Would / Be used to / Get used to — a quick reference before you start.

  • used to + infinitivea past habit or state that no longer happens or is true.
  • would + infinitivea repeated past action (not for past states — no "would be" for facts).
  • be used to + -ingaccustomed to something, familiar with it.
  • get used to + -ingbecoming accustomed to something, in the process of adjusting.
  • didn't use tonegative form of a past habit or state.
  • wasn't / weren't used to + -ingnot accustomed to something at that time.
