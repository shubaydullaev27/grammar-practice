1. Active or passive?

The same idea, two ways. In the passive, the thing that the action happens to comes first.

Remember
  • Singular subject → is / was. Plural subject → are / were.
  • Negative: isn’t, aren’t, wasn’t, weren’t (full forms is not, was not… are also correct).
  • Always use the past participle: The cake was made.  The cake was make.

4. When do we use the passive?

  • We don’t know who does the action: My phone was stolen on the bus.
  • It is obvious who does it: The thief was caught. (by the police)
  • It is not important who does it: Rubbish is collected every Wednesday.
by + who does the action The song was written by Taylor Swift.
Use by only when it is important.
in / on / at + place Oranges are grown in Spain.
Tickets are sold at the entrance.
