Grammar Summary

Wish & If only — we use both to talk about regrets, imaginary situations, or things we would like to be different.

  • wish + Past Simpleregret about a present situation.e.g. She wishes she had more free time.
  • wish + would + infinitivecomplaint about someone else's behaviour, or a desire for change — not your own actions (use "could" instead).e.g. I wish you would listen to me.
  • wish + had + past participleregret about a past event.e.g. I wish I had studied harder.
  • If onlysame meaning as "I wish", but expresses a stronger emotion.e.g. If only I were taller!
