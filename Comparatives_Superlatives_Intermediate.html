Grammar Summary

Comparatives & Superlatives

We use comparatives to compare two people or things, and superlatives to compare one with a whole group.

Structure
short adj + -er + than / more + long adj + than
tall → taller than · expensive → more expensive than
Bakhrom is taller than Sardor. → short adjective + -er
Samarkand is more interesting than Tashkent. → long adjective + more

Five patterns to know

-er / more

Comparing two things.

This phone is more expensive than that one.

less

Opposite of more.

Tashkent is less exciting than Samarkand.

as … as

Equal comparison (or not as … as).

Ali is as tall as Bek.

modifiers

much / far / a lot (big); a little / slightly / a bit (small).

This bag is slightly heavier than that one.

-est / most

Comparing one with a whole group.

Ali is the tallest student in the class.
