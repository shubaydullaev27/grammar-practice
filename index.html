Grammar Summary

Modal Verbs — a quick reference before you start.

  • muststrong obligation or certainty.
  • have toexternal obligation.
  • ought to / shouldadvice.
  • may / might / couldpossibility.
  • mustn'tprohibition.
  • don't have to / needn'tlack of necessity.
  • needn't have + past participlean unnecessary action that was completed.
  • can't have + past participlesomething was impossible in the past.
