Task 6 — Complete with a Participle Clause

3 items

Complete the second sentence so the meaning is similar to the first, using a participle clause.

e.g. A bag which contained over a million dollars was found during the raid on the house. → A bag containing over a million dollars was found during the raid on the house.
1We arrived late, so we were not allowed into the theatre.
, we were not allowed into the theatre.
2Because I'm not American, I really don't understand American football.
, I really don't understand American football.
3They were woken by a noise, so they ran outside to see what had happened.
, they ran outside to see what had happened.
